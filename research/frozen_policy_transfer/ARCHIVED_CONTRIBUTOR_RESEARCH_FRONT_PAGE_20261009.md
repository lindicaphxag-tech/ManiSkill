### Current research flagship: selective evidence queries, not blind ACK guesses

**[Research manuscript draft: *Command Execution as a Latent State*](research/frozen_policy_transfer/COMMAND_EXECUTION_LATENT_STATE_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md)** · [new 64-state prospective confirmation](research/frozen_policy_transfer/ROBUST_QUERY_NEW64_PROSPECTIVE_RESULTS.md). On **64 truly new frozen-PPO task seeds** in genuine ManiSkill PhysX, an evidence-gated two-history bounded controller with **selective trusted target reads** completed **60/64** while using **15 privileged reads**; mandatory one-read recovery completed **57/64** using **64 reads**.
