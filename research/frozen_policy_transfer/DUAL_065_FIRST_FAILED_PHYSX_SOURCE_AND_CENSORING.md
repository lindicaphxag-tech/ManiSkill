# First-run failure and nonexposure integrity disclosure

**Research integrity note · 2026-10-09.** This is an experimental implementation repair, **not** new positive physics evidence.

The original 64-state dual-criterion [pre-outcome frozen protocol](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/commit/44cdec26c2827edd7552d98f973482ba73d74433) preregistered PullCube 1820001–1820032 and StackCube 1830001–1830032, four native physical t2/t3 ACK truths each task, 10 separately stepped controller comparison worlds per seed, 0.65 source-normalized history-score floor selected from the earlier **training** cohort 176/177, and the requirement of identical actual pre-t5 target/achieved SE(3) for public/dual/fixed-read comparisons.

## Original first-run failure MUST stay visible

[First native physics CI #37926518190](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/actions/runs/37926518190) passed source preregistration checks but StackCube chunk3 failed. The actual native runner raised **Missing precommitted public t3 dispatched command** while processing the source original task/reset after five complete episode logs, identified as original new StackCube seed **1830030**. The public reference arm terminated before stepping its second registered unknown ACK; it was invalid for the mandatory two-fault *physical exposure* comparison. No model accuracy conclusion should be drawn from that exception. The failure artifact/log must not be discarded or characterized as a complete all64 validation.

## Exact bounded repair after observing implementation failure

- Original physics policy/control truth and 0.65 decision threshold **unchanged**; no prior samples or new seeds removed; no PPO tuning.
- When the public reference never dispatches native t3, the two specially paired comparator arms now **do not fabricate an unmatched t3 action**. They explicitly log `pre_t3_reference_censored` with seed, reason, original public fault steps, current refusal/failure and their original task-read outcomes.
- The first per-shard source auditor now scores all eight original seeds but separates **physically fully exposed and pre-t5 SE(3)-matched** from **pre-t3 censored**. The complete all64 independent auditor propagates both denominators and forbids printing `full 2x2 physically matched` when any original reset is nonexposed.
- This repair occurred **after the original failed run** and therefore invalidates an unqualified claim that subsequent results are the untouched first-attempt prospective 64. A later successful repair run is a reproducible amended implementation, with that limitation disclosed. It cannot silently promote censored samples into full-exposure evidence. No statistical error or noninferiority guarantee follows simply from CI green.

The scientific interpretation has two separate units: **64 original task-reset intent-to-treat states** (no seed deleted; includes early failures and censoring) and the **strictly audited actually full-ACK-exposed matched physical subset**, whose size must be reported as observed. Selecting only the latter and claiming it was a preregistered independent random cohort would be selection bias.

The long-term goal remains a genuinely prospective additional new-seed validation with a robust all-seed physical-exposure protocol, an independently calibrated model-validity signal and meaningful matched-resource competitor, not simply a stronger posthoc threshold.
