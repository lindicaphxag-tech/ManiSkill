# When Did My Command Execute? Causal Evidence for Latent Controller-Memory Authority

*Main-track-style research manuscript v3.0 · prospective findings + independently registered confirmation · 9 October 2026. Not peer reviewed; no hardware safety or external reproduction claimed.*

## Abstract

Frozen robot policies may issue valid Cartesian actions while the native target-integrating controller holds a different last-commanded target because an earlier action was executed without a corresponding acknowledgement. The latent memory cannot generally be reconstructed by simply substituting the achieved end-effector pose, and successful task completion need not imply correct execution-history inference. We investigate history-aware action authority using physically executed ManiSkill tasks driven by pretrained PullCube and StackCube PPO policies. After two unknown command execution events, up to four full SE(3) target histories remain plausible. A public achieved-motion witness can authorize a uniquely supported complete history or explicitly acquire the true commanded target; importantly, this empirical witness does not constitute collision/contact safety or a deterministic history-identification certificate. In a newly completed **same-reset 2×2 physical ACK factorial experiment**, 32 independent task/reset initial states are each evaluated under all four applied/held execution truths, with nine genuinely stepped native-controller worlds per condition (128 task×truth cells, 1,152 physical trajectories). The public method finishes **116/128** cells using **91** privileged target reads, compared with **108/128** and **109** reads for a strong task-preselected query regime and **109/128** with **128** reads for mandatory readback. Outcomes are paired over resets: public-only success occurs in 11 cells, comparator-only in 3. A 32-reset-cluster sign-swap sensitivity test gives a two-sided exploratory **p=0.055664**, not conventional evidence of superiority at 0.05. A separate fully matched physical study found a confidently incorrect complete controller history even when the robot successfully completed its task, and a newly completed 32-reset nonzero physical probe pilot did not outperform a zero-motion probe. We therefore distinguish task success, history correctness, actual privileged information cost and physical actuator intervention. We pre-register a **separate, unobserved 64-reset-cluster, 256-condition, 2,304-world confirmation**, with a practical success gain threshold of 4 percentage points and an independent seed-cluster inference gate. This work constitutes an increasingly stringent simulator research program, not yet an established superior task-learning algorithm or third-party adopted benchmark.

## 1. Introduction

An action contract includes its coordinate chart, controller state-transition memory and the epistemic status of action execution. In a native achieved-relative controller, a policy may reasonably interpret a Cartesian delta against the measured current pose. In a commanded-target-relative controller, the same numeric delta may be integrated against an internally accumulated target that differs from physical achieved pose. With missing or delayed execution acknowledgements, observing a policy's requested action sequence does not identify which prior command modified target memory. We isolate this ambiguous-execution transition, distinct from identifying an unknown action mapping (studied in existing online system-identification and ActionShift/DualABI).

An information-cost reduction is scientifically useful only if it is achieved without hiding errors in latent state, prematurely dropped tasks, re-used initial states, or extra free physical probes. Prior experiments demonstrate that three attractive shortcuts fail: (i) a geometric setpoint certificate does not guarantee task-level success when query timing is late; (ii) completed manipulation may conceal an incorrectly authorized full controller target; and (iii) a stronger physical probe may cost more without identifying more latent target histories.

**What is supported today:** (a) a controlled same-reset factorial design testing all four physically realized ACK outcomes, (b) paired task-success and true private getter counts on genuine native PPO trajectories, and (c) a source-preserving hard-negative dataset revealing mistaken confident authority despite task completion. **What is not established:** statistical or causal superiority of public authority, learned policy generalization, actual unknown TCP/middleware packet-loss recovery, physical robot safety, and independent external reproduction.

## 2. Formal task: hidden execution with a known controller map

Let s_t be achieved robot/environment state, M_t the internally commanded target pose, a_t the frozen PPO Cartesian action and z_t∈{0,1} the actual commit of a delivered native command. With a documented controller transition F, the target memory evolves as M_{t+1}=F(M_t,a_t) when z_t=1, and remains M_t when z_t=0. Observations include achieved motion and source-policy measurements but not z_t or authoritative target M_t unless a priced readback is performed. Unknown acknowledgements at t2 and t3 yield four candidate memory histories H_t={HH,AH,HA,AA}, where a candidate records a complete target SE(3) pose, not independent Euler components.

The online decision is one of: **authorize an existing complete candidate**, **take one target-memory readback**, **issue a documented physical information probe**, or **refuse unsafe/unsupported execution**. Each candidate's controller chart must be physically representable and all history-source/provenance assumptions fresh. No public sensor may see audit-only true target getters or the physical z_t truth. Distinguish: (1) official PPO task success Y, (2) history correctness when authorized, (3) counted privileged getter reads Q, (4) publicly sampled XYZ events and physical probe steps, (5) controller action validity and intervention exposure.

### Information-theoretic impossibility boundary (standard binary testing result, specialized here)

If two latent target histories h0,h1 induce public-observation distributions P0,P1 under an identical predetermined probe, the minimum wrong-history decision probability under a balanced binary prior, when forced to choose without readback, is (1−TV(P0,P1))/2. This follows from the classical Bayes binary testing identity and is **not a new theorem**. When P0=P1, no history estimator seeing only that public probe can exceed 50% identification accuracy on the balanced pair, regardless of neural model capacity; a trusted readback or an intervention changing the observation distributions is needed for stronger reliability. For four histories, the same lower bound holds on any difficult two-history subproblem after conditioning on that pair. This directly motivates abstention and active observability rather than interpreting empirical residual uniqueness as a universal guarantee.

## 3. Baseline method and genuine design constraints

At a known-delivered native t4 neutral probe, the public method compares achieved xyz motion to projected motion toward each complete candidate history using the original empirically estimated task-specific response envelope. It authorizes the unique candidate only if the winner lies within the frozen epsilon and all alternatives exceed epsilon plus margin; otherwise it charges an explicit trusted target read. The full orientation is inherited from the selected candidate history. Both original source and third-party released PPO checkpoint hashes are fixed before simulation.

The **strong task-only comparator** was chosen from older *development* results and fixed before this new study: PullCube uses the reactive query-on-refusal policy, StackCube uses early target readback. We do not pretend this simple comparator is newly learned. Mandatory target read and zero-query always-held controls provide resource/success anchors. All arms have actual native env.step executions, not trajectories synthesized by adding action deltas offline.

## 4. Prospective factorial experiment: reset identity × native executed ACK truth

The first original complete run [37932361044](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/actions/runs/37932361044) registered 32 distinct original task/reset seeds (16 PullCube, 16 StackCube), four physical truth conditions per exact task/reset ID (HH,AH,HA,AA), and nine native physical controller treatments per cell: **32×4×9 = 1,152 actual simulation worlds**. The experiment publishes the complete original pre-step numerical source policy observation vector and original achieved XYZ/quaternion for all four conditions. A source checker requires every quartet to align within the PRE-FROZEN componentwise absolute tolerance 5×10^−5, while reporting bitwise SHA equality separately; near-numerical equivalence must not be advertised as byte-identical initial physical state.

The independent original-source auditor refuses missing shards, task/reset identities, illegal native truth dispatch, missing public sensor samples or private getter accounting, violations of source PPO/blob identity, and the loss of original negative tasks or premature intervention refusals. The statistical unit is the **task/reset seed cluster with four associated fault outcomes**, not each of the 128 condition cells and certainly not each of the 1,152 control worlds. Explore a paired two-sided sign-swap test at the 32-cluster level and seed-cluster bootstrap uncertainty; the sign-swap p-value requires a symmetry/exchangeability assumption and is not equivalent to a randomized controlled clinical/robot trial.

## 5. Original results

| Method, all physically stepped | Native task successes per 128 truth cells | Counted private target reads |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Complete-history public motion witness or readback** | **116/128 (90.63%)** | **91** |
| Strong fixed task-conditioned query policy | 108/128 (84.38%) | 109 |
| Mandatory true target readback | 109/128 (85.16%) | 128 |
| Always assume both unknown commands were held | 71/128 (55.47%) | 0 |

Against the preselected strong task policy, the public method has **105 jointly successful**, **9 jointly failed**, **11 public-only success**, and **3 comparator-only success** conditions. The observed difference is **+8/128 = +6.25 percentage points** in paired condition-level task success and **18 fewer counted authoritative target reads**. Its real sensor and t4 probe costs must be considered separately: equal native probe assumptions only apply to arms that physically reach that step. The 32-reset paired sign-swap sensitivity is **p=0.0556640625** two-sided, which crosses neither a prespecified significance barrier nor the threshold for definitive population generalization. Neither 128 cells nor 1,152 physically stepped controller worlds are 128/1,152 independent reset clusters. **One positive cohort does not prove a learned algorithm breakthrough.**

## 6. Hard negative controls and failure analysis

**Wrong yet successful latent state:** in the separate original [640-world matched-public PhysX evidence](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/tree/main/research/frozen_policy_transfer/evidence/matched_public_bayes_original640_1760001_1770032), PullCube seed 1760020 physically executed both uncertain native commands. The historical observer confidently chose history index 0 but the actual target corresponded to candidate 3 (audit-only target mismatch 67.6mm, 0.0536rad). All compared arms completed the task. There was **one wrong among 16 confident candidate authorizations**: a calibrated per-history risk must be assessed independently of official binary task success.

**No free information from bigger probe:** a source-frozen 32 fresh reset/352 genuine native-world study [37932453422](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/actions/runs/37932453422) tested a nonzero physical t4 probe against zero native probe in the same applied/held conditions. Both arms succeeded on **28/32 tasks**. Nonzero probe used **28 privileged reads** and authorized 4 histories; zero probe used **26 reads** and authorized 6. No observed wrong confidence in that small cohort. Therefore stronger movement cannot be assumed to increase the information value or task utility of a probe.

**Calibration and OOD failure:** empirically enlarging tolerance did not improve old task successes and increased getter counts in a separate 64-real-PhysX study; a known t1 public kinematic model-validity check under changed joint drive strength failed to detect half the true response mismatches while rejecting many valid cases. These results prohibit claims of universal safety from one-time confidence thresholds or a past valid probe.

## 7. Independently registered confirmatory trial (pending, no result claimed)

The new protocol [CONFIRMATORY_FOUR_TRUTH_NEW64CLUSTERS_PREOUTCOME_V1.json](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/blob/research/confirmatory-new64clusters-factorial-20261009/research/CONFIRMATORY_FOUR_TRUTH_NEW64CLUSTERS_PREOUTCOME_V1.json) fixed completely unseen task/reset IDs PullCube 3210001–3210032 and StackCube 3220001–3220032 before the new experimental run. Exactly **64 independent reset clusters×4 physical truths×9 native strategies=2,304 planned original physical controller worlds** are registered. The method/classifier checkpoints, original native source and numeric initial tolerance remain frozen. One primary contrast is public motion authority vs strongest task-preselected query. A **practically relevant gain of ≥4 percentage points** and a **two-sided 64-cluster exact sign-swap descriptive p≤0.05** were declared before new outcome access. This is a separate test; development32 is never pooled into its primary inference.

Execution integrity note: the first workflow exposed an argument-parsing range bug when increasing chunks from two to four; chunks 2–3 were rejected before physical stepping. The CLI-only correction was separately documented, and previous partial original shards will not be counted as an independent cohort. The [explicit amendment](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/blob/research/confirmatory-new64clusters-factorial-20261009/research/CONFIRMATORY_NEW64_EXECUTION_AMENDMENT_ORIGINAL_FAILURE.md) explains why a fully clean first attempt cannot be claimed and why all original negative outcomes must be retained. **Until 32/32 shards and the complete 64-cluster audit pass, this confirmatory study has no experimental result.**

## 8. Prior art and actual novelty constraints

Hidden action semantics, online system identification, rapid motor adaptation, active belief probing and task regret are established topics. ActionShift DualABI is directly relevant, and a thresholded normalized residual score trained from prior physical observations already matched the full-history method within **one** actual getter on another 64-state matched-info cohort. Selective risk/coverage intervals and multiplicity-corrected binomial testing are standard, not newly invented control theory. This study's distinct task definition is **unknown physical execution history of an otherwise known, stateful target-relative controller**, together with the right to preserve full target provenance and charge authoritative memory readings. A competitive robot-learning submission must introduce and validate genuinely learned, task-effective observability/utility, rather than rely on a static history threshold plus more simulator seeds.

## 9. Limitations and publication-grade missing gates

These results use author-operated ManiSkill CPU PhysX and two released frozen PPOs that share one Panda native controller family; a scripted xArm6 correction study does not provide independent learned manipulation task recovery on another robot. Injecting true native action HOLD/APPLY is not the same as dropped or delayed real TCP/ROS ACKs. Contact/force safety, out-of-distribution sensor calibration, fault timing, true wallclock cost, public frames and physical probe magnitude are not generally controlled across every historical policy branch. There is no independent external investigator physical/simulation replication, official ActionShift DualABI matched active controller experiment, robust state-conditioned risk guarantee, or full closed-loop frozen SmolVLA fault-recovery success. All negative cases and CI/amendment failures remain part of the artifact history.

**The scientific next mechanism:** learn a task-conditioned public response *discriminability* or task-value-of-information function from physically grounded, cluster-separated data; under uncertain execution choose an informative bounded probe, one real target query, a complete history authorization, or explicit refusal. Train on older clusters, calibrate on disjoint clusters, and evaluate a frozen decision model on genuinely novel robot task episodes against matched public information and physical control costs. A successful result must exceed task-ID query and strong prior-trained score-only baselines, retain conditional latent history error control **at nontrivial coverage**, and survive a second policy-capable controller family and outside execution.
