# What cannot be claimed from the new 1152-world seed-matched native PhysX factorial

This is a **second, source-only** re-analysis of all original native PhysX data from the successful fresh numeric-parity 128-cell experiment [37932361044](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/ManiSkill/actions/runs/37932361044). The original 36-file SHA256 physical source archive was independently rechecked **before** this analysis. No additional task policy rollout happened in this audit.

It reports actual six-axis motor commands dispatched at both unknown ACK fault steps by the public method and matched source-seed task-aware/fixed comparators, not merely nominal APPLIED/HELD labels. Every task/truth cell is included even when the physical prefix differs between compared algorithms. It independently verifies actual known-delivered t4 neutral probe exposure and **observed** extra public achieved XYZ sample events. At least two original public policies refused EARLY before their second ACK fault and their neutral probe; counting 128 identical probes or 256 achieved XYZ sample events would therefore be false. The intent-to-treat denominator stays all 128, and only truly co-exposed action pairs enter pre-query motor parity denominators.

When actual native t3 actions differ BEFORE public private-target-read decision, the end-to-end task outcome contrast cannot be attributed *only* to different information acquisition. The prior 64-state physically matched-prefix plus same-compiler experiment is the cleaner information-only result. This 128-cell experiment establishes 4-truth within-seed robustness and a positive descriptive end-to-end comparison, not a pure query-only causal treatment, noninferiority, p<.05 superiority, genuine network packet loss, VLA fault recovery, or outside-lab reproduction.
